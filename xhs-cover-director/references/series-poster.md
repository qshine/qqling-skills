# Poster series planning

Read this reference when the user asks for a carousel, 图文海报, 多页海报, or more than one coordinated image.

## Deliverable contract

- Default to five pages total: one cover and four content pages.
- Accept an explicit request for one to five total pages. Never interpret “five images” as a cover plus five more images.
- Use 1080 x 1920 for every page and one visual system across the set.
- Produce the exact publishing caption at the same time as the page copy.

## Plan the editorial arc

Write private notes for reader, payoff, tension, and evidence boundary before drafting. Give every page one job and one new piece of information.

Use this default five-page arc, adapting the middle pages to the source:

| Page | Default job | Copy shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cover hook | one short headline, one emphasis phrase, one or two relevant emoji |
| 2 | Problem or recognition | a concrete pain point and why it matters |
| 3 | Core solution | the central idea, method, or change |
| 4 | Evidence, features, or how-to | one to three useful specifics rather than a feature dump |
| 5 | Conclusion, boundary, or action | takeaway, limitation, safety note, or natural next action |

For fewer pages, merge adjacent jobs without shrinking paragraphs to unreadable sizes. The first page remains the cover; every later page uses the `content` role.

## Write distinct page copy

- Draft several cover hooks and score them with [headline-selection.md](headline-selection.md). Keep only the best supported option.
- Prefer roughly 8–18 Chinese characters for the cover headline and 6–14 for a content-page heading.
- Give a content page one heading plus one to three short points. Aim for roughly 20–60 Chinese characters total when accuracy allows.
- Do not repeat the cover as the title of a content page. Repeated product names or necessary terms are acceptable; repeated editorial claims are not.
- Keep meaningful English, numbers, punctuation, and capitalization exact.
- Use one source-supported platform phrase at most, such as “神器”, “避坑”, or “保姆级”. Do not claim “亲测”, “封神”, “必看”, or guaranteed outcomes without evidence.
- Treat emoji as cover-level visual cues, not evidence. Put one emoji sequence in each `emoji_stickers` item; normally use one or two stickers on the cover and none on dense content pages.

## Write the publishing caption

Write one caption of at most 300 Unicode characters including line breaks and hashtags. Include:

1. a concise opening hook;
2. the concrete reader payoff;
3. a material limitation or safety boundary when the source contains one;
4. a natural save, comment, try, or read action when appropriate;
5. three to five relevant hashtags.

Do not reproduce all five page texts in the caption. Do not browse for real-time trending words unless the user explicitly asks for current trends.

## Art-direct the series

- Select black-yellow warning, warm personal IP, beige scrapbook, or hand-drawn editorial from [visual-system.md](visual-system.md).
- Lock the palette, line weight, texture, lighting logic, and one recurring motif before generating page one.
- Change the focal object, diagram, or scene to match each page's job. Do not reuse one background five times with different text.
- Keep 20–30% of every page visually quiet for exact copy. Reserve larger quiet space on the cover.
- Keep all generated surfaces free of pseudo-text. Blank cards, bubbles, labels, notebook pages, and screens are allowed.
- If a portrait is supplied, use it on the cover or the one page where it adds meaning. Do not generate five identity variants unless the user explicitly requests repeated portrait use.
